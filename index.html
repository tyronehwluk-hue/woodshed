    Getting accurate counts
    • Use Chrome or Safari, allow the microphone, and put your phone or laptop about a metre from the guitar.
    • Strum all the strings of the chord once and let it ring for half a second before switching.
    • A change only counts when the new chord sounds right. If a string is muted or buzzing, the page tells you what it heard instead.
    • Tune up first. A badly out-of-tune guitar can confuse it.
