Tips for accurate counting
  • Allow the microphone and keep your phone or laptop about a metre from the guitar.
  • Strum the whole chord once and let it ring for half a second before switching.
  • A change only counts when the new chord sounds right. If a string is muted or buzzing, you'll see what the mic heard instead.
  • Tune up first. An out-of-tune guitar can confuse it.
